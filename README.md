# Screw Jack – 3D Modelling and Assembly

## Overview

A mechanical design mini-project involving the 3D modelling and assembly of a screw jack using Autodesk Fusion 360.

## Components

- Body
- Nut
- Screw Spindle
- Cup
- Washer
- Screw
- Tommy Bar

## Work Completed

- Parametric 3D modelling of components
- Complete assembly modelling
- Assembly and individual part drawings
- Application of limits, fits and tolerances
- Study of power screw mechanics

## Software

Autodesk Fusion 360

## Future Scope

- Stress and buckling analysis
- Finite Element Analysis (FEA)
- Material selection
- Etc
